How it works

Write it down

Open the book and type whatever you need to say. No one else ever sees it.

Hold to burn

Press and hold the button. The page catches, curls, and burns away in front of you.

It’s gone

The words are wiped the moment the fire reaches the top. There is no undo, and that’s the point.

Where it goes: nowhere

Everything you type stays in your browser, on your device, for as long as that one page is open. It is never sent anywhere, never written to a file, never backed up. Close the tab and there is nothing left to find. It works with your wi-fi switched off.

  • No account
  • No cloud
  • No analytics
  • No undo
  • Works offline

Say something

The book itself still keeps nothing. This box is separate — only what you type here and choose to send reaches Jake, so he knows whether it helped or what to fix. No name required, nothing else collected.
