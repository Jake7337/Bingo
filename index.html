The apps

Bingo Caller

Puts a full 75-ball game up on your TV. Master board, 17 patterns, your pace — you still call the numbers out loud. Free, so the money a bingo night raises goes to the cause instead of to software.

Burn Book

Write down whatever is eating at you, hold the button, and watch the page go up in flames. Nothing is saved. Nothing ever leaves your device.

Just Pick Something

“What do you want for dinner?” “I don’t know, what do you want?” One button ends that. It names one ordinary dinner — hit it again if you don’t like it. No recipes, no questions, no account.
