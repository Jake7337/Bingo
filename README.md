# Free Apps That Help

Small, single-file web apps that each do one useful thing. Made with AI and given away — no sign-up, no tracking, nothing to install.

**Live:** https://jake7337.github.io/Bingo/

## What's here

| App | What it does | Open | Info |
|-----|--------------|------|------|
| **Bingo Caller** | Puts a full 75-ball game on your TV — master board, 17 patterns, your pace. Silent by design; you call the numbers. Free for any group. | [/bingo/](https://jake7337.github.io/Bingo/bingo/) | [/bingo/info.html](https://jake7337.github.io/Bingo/bingo/info.html) |
| **Burn Book** | Write out what's eating at you, hold the button, watch it burn. Nothing is saved, nothing leaves your device. | [/burn-book/](https://jake7337.github.io/Bingo/burn-book/) | [/burn-book/info.html](https://jake7337.github.io/Bingo/burn-book/info.html) |
| **Just Pick Something** | "What do you want for dinner?" "I don't know, what do you want?" One button names one ordinary dinner. No recipes, no questions. | [/dinner/](https://jake7337.github.io/Bingo/dinner/) | [/dinner/info.html](https://jake7337.github.io/Bingo/dinner/info.html) |

Every app is one self-contained HTML file — no build step, no dependencies. Download the file and it works offline, forever.
